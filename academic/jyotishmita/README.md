## 📚 About Dr. Jyotismita Das

Dr. Jyotismita Das is an academic, wildlife researcher, and author specializing in:
- **Avian Ecology & Ornithology**: Research on threatened wetland species including the Greater and Lesser Adjutant Storks (*Leptoptilos dubius* and *L. javanicus*) across Ramsar sites like Deepor Beel, Assam.
- **Wildlife Conservation & Biodiversity**: Field studies on wetland ecosystems, community ecology, and habitat preservation in Northeast India.
- **Environmental Biology & Biostatistics**: Teaching core undergraduate and postgraduate curricula under NEP guidelines.
- **Published Textbooks**: Co-author of *AECC — Environmental Studies* (NEP Curriculum standard textbook).
