01 · Academic Profile

Rooted in field ecology & biodiversity science.

Connecting empirical field biology with modern quantitative biostatistics to safeguard fragile ecological ecosystems in Assam and Northeast India.

From the Ramsar wetland of Deepor Beel to the riverine habitats of Kolong, her research decodes species survival, habitat pressures and ecological resilience.

Her doctoral thesis established groundbreaking insights on the seasonal diversity of waterbirds and diurnal activity budgeting of the Large Whistling Teal (Dendrocygna bicolor). Her expanding portfolio encompasses Lesser Adjutant Stork nesting ecology, aquatic microplastics, riverine fishery potential and environmental quality indicators.

03 · Key Domains

Core Research Themes

A multi-disciplinary agenda uniting field ornithology, wetland restoration, eco-toxicology and quantitative biostatistics.

01

Avian Ecology & Behavioral Budgets

Waterbird diversity, diurnal feeding strategies, activity budgeting, and nesting dynamics of species such as the Lesser Adjutant Stork.

02

Wetland Biodiversity & Ramsar Sites

Comprehensive ecological surveys of Assam's Ramsar sites including Deepor Beel, tracking seasonal species composition and habitat degradation.

03

Wildlife Conservation

Identifying anthropogenic pressures, habitat fragmentation, conservation threats, and mitigation frameworks for threatened species.

04

Environmental Biology & Toxicology

Water quality parameters, aquatic insect assemblages, river pollution in the Kolong River system, and microplastic contamination.

05

Biostatistics & Ecological Modeling

Applying rigorous statistical modeling, diversity indices, and quantitative analysis to biological research and fauna surveys.

06

Community & Sustainability

Connecting ecological conservation with local communities through Assam State Biodiversity Board People's Biodiversity Registers (PBRs).

Featured Field Research

Deepor Beel & Aquatic Avifauna Ecology
